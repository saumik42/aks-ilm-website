The story behind AKS Ilm

One question.
A growing journey.

The journey behind AKS Ilm began on April 8, 2023, during Ramadan, with a simple idea that came to mind: why not make learning about Islam a little more fun and interactive, not only for myself, but for others too? As Muslims, we are one Ummah, and beneficial knowledge is something worth learning and sharing together.

Looking back, none of this would have been possible without Allah’s guidance and permission. What began as a small idea was something Allah enabled me to pursue and gradually develop in ways I had never originally envisioned. Alhamdulillah for every step of the journey.

From Instagram to a shared habit

It all started with something simple: one multiple-choice Islamic trivia question at a time on Instagram. Alhamdulillah, people seemed to genuinely enjoy taking part, and I enjoyed creating the questions just as much. What I originally thought would be a Ramadan-only idea soon began to feel like something worth continuing, especially after friends encouraged me to keep it going.

And so, the questions kept coming. Some weeks brought more than others, but there was never a need to rush it. The idea remained simple: one question at a time, learn something beneficial and share it with others.

Giving the questions a permanent home

The more questions I created, the more I found myself enjoying the process behind them. Researching and verifying what I shared often meant learning something new myself, and sometimes a question that began as something for others ended up teaching me too. What started as a fun way to make Islamic learning engaging gradually became another way for me to reflect, strengthen my understanding of the Deen, and grow in my own journey as a Muslim.

Eventually, the collection reached a point where I wanted the questions, answers, and references to have a permanent home rather than live only on Instagram. I wanted people to be able to go back, revisit older questions, and learn at their own pace. That led to the creation of the Islamic Trivia Library in late 2025, which is why AKS Ilm carries Est. 2025.

Growing with the same purpose

Since then, Alhamdulillah, what started with those simple Instagram questions has gradually grown into AKS Ilm, a broader Islamic learning resource. The Islamic Trivia Library remains at its heart, while interactive challenges, Islamic terminology, significant places connected to Islam, beneficial reminders, and other features have given people more ways to learn, explore, and reflect.

Through every stage, the purpose has remained the same: to make seeking beneficial Islamic knowledge engaging, accessible, and something we can share with one another. Whatever good comes from AKS Ilm is only by the permission and guidance of Allah, and I am grateful that He has enabled a small Ramadan idea to grow into what it is today.

May Allah accept this effort, place barakah in it, allow it to benefit others, guide it toward what pleases Him, and forgive any shortcomings. Ameen.

About This Resource

AKS Ilm is an independently created and maintained educational resource dedicated to sharing beneficial Islamic knowledge from established sources. Its name reflects this purpose, as Ilm (علم) means ‘knowledge’ in Arabic. AKS Ilm is not a source of religious rulings or a substitute for qualified scholarship. And Allah knows best.

The Purpose

The purpose of AKS Ilm is simple: to encourage the seeking and sharing of beneficial Islamic knowledge in an engaging and accessible way. The Islamic Trivia Library remains at its heart, while each feature offers another opportunity to learn, reflect, and benefit, InshaAllah.

Behind the Content

Sharing knowledge about Islam comes with a responsibility, and that is something I try to keep in mind with everything I create for AKS Ilm. I take care to research what I share, referring back to the Qur’an, authentic Hadith, and established Islamic sources where appropriate. I am still a student of knowledge myself, so the process is not only about sharing with others, but also about continuing to learn, reflect, and grow in my understanding of the Deen, InshaAllah.

How Content Comes Together

Each piece of content begins with an idea, followed by research and careful verification before it is prepared and shared on AKS Ilm.

New trivia questions are first shared on Instagram, then added to the Islamic Trivia Library with their answers. The collection is updated regularly as new questions are prepared and shared.
